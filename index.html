The Narrative: Cultivating Code Across Borders

My engineering journey is a story of growth across two continents. It began in Pune, India, where I first learned to turn complex financial data into visual stories at Neuronode. This curiosity led me to Capgemini, where I spent two years mastering the discipline of Java and enterprise systems, eventually cutting manual workflows by 50% for global brands like McDonald’s.

In 2024, I took a leap of faith and moved to Germany to pursue my Master’s at TU Chemnitz. This transition pushed me to bridge the gap between cultures and technologies from developing AI-driven accessibility tools at AI2Connect to my current role at EEX Group. Today in Leipzig, I engineer automation frameworks that ensure 100% accuracy for the European energy market.

"I don’t just build software; I cultivate resilient solutions that grow with the people who use them."
